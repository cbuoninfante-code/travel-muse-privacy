# Privacy Policy for Destination Muse

Last updated: May 31, 2026

## Overview

Destination Muse is a travel itinerary app built by Christina Buoninfante. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

Destination Muse does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

All itinerary data is stored locally on your device. We do not have servers, user accounts, or databases that store your information.

## Subscriptions

Destination Muse offers an optional monthly subscription processed entirely through Apple In-App Purchases. We do not handle or store your payment information. All billing is managed by Apple. For questions about billing, please visit your Apple ID settings or contact Apple Support.

## Third-Party Services

Destination Muse does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions and can be found at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## Children's Privacy

Destination Muse does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not collect any personal information from any user.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the new version at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
